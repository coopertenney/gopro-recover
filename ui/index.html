GoPro Recover

Gets deleted or formatted videos and photos back off a GoPro SD card. The card is only ever read.

Insert the card

Put the SD card in a reader and plug it in. If your Mac offers to initialise it, click Ignore.

You'll be asked for your Mac password once so the card can be read directly.
ffmpeg wasn't found, so previews won't work. Close this and run the launcher again, or install ffmpeg.

Scan the card

Reads the whole card once and lists every recording it can still find. About 12 minutes for a 64 GB card.

What are you looking for?

Narrow the list, or leave everything open to see it all.

Camera clocks on GoPros reset when the battery is pulled, so dates can be years off. Length and resolution are exact.

Results

Click Preview on a clip to see a sheet of frames from start to end. Tick the ones you want, then recover them.

Recovering

Rebuilding the files from the card into your chosen folder.
